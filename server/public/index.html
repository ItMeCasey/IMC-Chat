TWITCH'S OWN PLAYER

Choose Watch with: Twitch's own player above and the tab's play button opens Twitch's real web player in a window of this app. If you have Twitch Turbo, or you subscribe to the channel, you will not see ads once you are logged in there. It is the same login as Connect Twitch: connecting the chat opens its approval page in this window, so you log in once for both. The app never sees your password: you type it on Twitch's own page.
